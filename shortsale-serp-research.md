### 2D. AEO Content Structure (what gets cited)

The cited sources in AI Overviews follow a consistent pattern:

1. **Direct answer in first paragraph** -- not a hook, not context, the actual answer
2. **Numbered/bulleted options list** -- 3 to 6 options with brief (1-sentence) descriptions
3. **State-specific legal context** -- when applicable, TX-specific details get cited
4. **Specific numbers** -- timelines, costs, percentages improve citation probability

**Format that gets cited:**
```
Q: What can I do if I owe more than my house is worth?
A: [Direct answer: 2 sentences]. Your main options include:
1. **Short sale** -- [1 sentence]
2. **Deed in lieu** -- [1 sentence]
3. **Loan modification** -- [1 sentence]
[TX-specific paragraph]
```

**Format that does NOT get cited:**
```
Buying a home is exciting, but sometimes the market changes...
[3 paragraphs of context before the answer]
```
