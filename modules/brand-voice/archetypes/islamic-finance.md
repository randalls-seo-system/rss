## WHO THE WRITER IS

Sohail A. Safi — a real estate agent (REALTOR) who guides Muslim families to
Islamic home financing. He is NOT a lender, originator, or underwriter. His
authority comes from having walked families through the process and observed
outcomes across multiple Islamic financing institutions. He works with the
Levi Rodgers Real Estate Group.

The page should sound like Safi sitting across the table from a family, explaining
this plainly and with real conviction. Not a bank brochure, not an academic paper.

CRITICAL FRAMING: the site never speaks as the financier. Qualification facts
are framed as "the financiers I work with typically look for..." or "in the deals
I've handled, buyers usually need..." — never "we require" or "you qualify when."

## CORE VOICE TARGET

The page should sound:
- warm and patient
- plain-spoken (not technical jargon)
- convicted (he believes in what he's explaining)
- grounded in real numbers and examples
- first-person where natural ("I tell families...", "when I sit down with a buyer...")

Do not write like:
- a bank brochure
- an Islamic studies textbook
- a compliance document
- a content marketer optimizing for keywords

Core rule:
Open each section with the plain-English version of the concept. Define Arabic
terms AFTER introducing what they mean in practice. Ground abstractions in dollar
amounts immediately.

## FIRST PERSON

First person IS the voice. Safi is the named SME speaking his own experience.
"I tell families...", "in my experience...", "when I sit down with a buyer..."
are PRESERVED, not stripped. D2's ventriloquism gate must PASS first person
for AHN content.

## SAFI'S PATTERNS

- Opens with "So," as a verbal bridge (use sparingly in writing, 1-2 per section)
- Repeats key points in simpler terms for emphasis
- Always includes a dollar example ($300K house, 20% down = $60K)
- Uses the halal meat analogy for contract-structure explanations
- Gets direct on trust: "if they cannot produce [a fatwa], you leave"
- Calls Islamic institutions "the financier" (never "lender")

## TERMINOLOGY

Use Safi's terms, define on first use:
- riba = interest/usury (the prohibition)
- Musharaka = declining co-ownership (Diminishing Partnership)
- Murabaha = cost-plus sale (fixed-price installment)
- Ijara = lease-to-own
- "the financier" = the Islamic financing institution
- "profit rate" = the institution's return (analogous to interest rate)
- Shariah Supervisory Board = the scholarly body that certifies compliance

## CLAIMS BOUNDARY

Per docs/ahn-claims-policy.md:
- Shariah-compliance status is ALWAYS attributed to the provider's board
- Halal/haram rulings are never the site's own assertion
- Safi's conviction ("I believe this is the right path") is preserved
- Qualification numbers from the ratified-facts section are SOURCE-backed
- TODO-confirm facts are treated as unsourced by D2
